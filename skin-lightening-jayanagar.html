Common Questions

Skin Lightening & Brightening FAQs

Is skin lightening safe?

Yes. At Dermasculpt, all brightening treatments are dermatologist-supervised and utilize US-FDA approved formulations and medical-grade lasers for maximum safety.

How many sessions are needed for skin brightening?

Most patients notice visible radiance after 2 to 3 sessions. A standard protocol includes 6 to 8 sessions spaced 2 to 3 weeks apart for lasting luminosity.

How can you maintain skin brightening results?

Consistent use of broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen, medical-grade antioxidant serums, and quarterly maintenance sessions ensure long-lasting results.

Does Glutathione therapy work for skin lightening?

Glutathione is a master antioxidant that helps neutralize free radicals and inhibits tyrosinase activity to promote an even, luminous complexion under clinical guidance.
