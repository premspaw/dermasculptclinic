What is Hair Transplant surgery? Hair transplant is a micro-surgical procedure that involves moving hair follicles from a 'donor site' (usually the back of the head) to a 'recipient site' (the thinning or bald areas). At Dermasculpt Clinic in Jayanagar, Bangalore, led by expert dermatologist Dr. Deepak Devakar, we specialize in the advanced FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) technique. This method ensures a natural-looking hairline, maximum graft survival, and a virtually painless experience with no visible linear scarring. Our dedicated surgical team focuses on precision and density to restore not just your hair, but your confidence as well.

Clinical Care Protocol

What to Expect: Preparation, Session & Recovery

STEP 01

Pre-Care Preparation

  • Comprehensive trichoscopy mapping to verify donor zone density and graft requirements.
  • Avoid aspirin, blood thinners, and vitamin E supplements for 5 days (under physician guidance).
  • Stop smoking and alcohol consumption 3 days before surgery to optimize microcirculation.
  • Wash scalp with prescribed antiseptic shampoo on the morning of the procedure.
STEP 02

During the Procedure

  • Local anesthesia administration ensures completely pain-free harvesting and implantation.
  • Micro-motorized 0.75mm–0.85mm punch extraction of intact follicular units (FUE).
  • Direct Hair Transplant (DHT) slit creation following natural hair angle and density.
  • Assisted graft placement in high-magnification stereoscopic optical fields.
STEP 03

Post-Care & Recovery

  • Sleep with head elevated at 45 degrees for the first 3 nights to minimize forehead swelling.
  • Spray sterile saline mist every 2 hours on transplanted grafts during waking hours.
  • Do not touch, scratch, or scrub grafts; scabs shed naturally between days 7 and 10.
  • New hair growth initiates at month 3–4, with full dense maturation by month 9–12.

Common Questions

Hair Transplant FAQs

What is FUE hair transplant?

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a minimally invasive hair restoration technique where individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into the thinning regions.

Is hair transplant permanent?

Yes, the transplanted hair is harvested from the permanent zone (back of the head) and is genetically resistant to thinning, providing lifelong results.

How much does a hair transplant cost in Jayanagar?

The cost depends on the number of grafts required. At Dermasculpt, we offer transparent pricing starting from ₹45,000, with detailed consultations by Dr. Devakar.

How long is the recovery?

Most patients can return to work within 3-5 days. The transplanted hair starts growing after 3 months, with full results visible in 9-12 months.
