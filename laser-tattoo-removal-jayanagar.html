Expert Tattoo Removal Process

Removing a tattoo is a journey that requires precision and the right technology. At Dermasculpt, we use Q-switched lasers that emit high-energy light in extremely short pulses. This energy is absorbed by the tattoo ink, breaking it into tiny fragments that are then naturally cleared by your body's immune system.

Common Questions

Laser Tattoo Removal FAQs

How many sessions are needed for tattoo removal?

Most amateur tattoos require 4 to 6 sessions, while professional multi-colored tattoos may take 6 to 10 sessions spaced 6 to 8 weeks apart for complete fading.

Is laser tattoo removal painful?

Most patients describe the sensation as similar to the snap of a rubber band. We apply topical numbing cream before the session to maximize comfort.

Does laser tattoo removal leave a scar?

Our advanced Q-switched laser targets only the ink pigment without damaging surrounding skin tissue, minimizing the risk of scarring.

Can all tattoo colors be removed?

Black, dark blue, and red pigments respond fastest. Modern multi-wavelength lasers can also effectively treat green, sky blue, and stubborn mixed inks.
