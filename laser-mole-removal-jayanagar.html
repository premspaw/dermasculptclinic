Precision Laser Removal

Moles are common skin growths, but when they are unwanted or located in prominent areas, laser removal is the most effective and aesthetic solution. Our CO₂ laser allows for extreme precision, vaporizing the mole tissue layer by layer until it is flush with the skin, promoting natural healing with virtually no trace left behind.

The procedure is straightforward and fast. After a quick consultation and skin analysis by Dr. Devakar, the area is numbed with a local anesthetic. There is no need for stitches or complex aftercare.

Clinical Care Protocol

What to Expect: Preparation, Session & Recovery

STEP 01

Pre-Care Preparation

  • Dermoscopic assessment by Dr. Deepak Devakar to verify benign cosmetic nature of the lesion.
  • Avoid picking, scratching, or applying over-the-counter mole removal acids.
  • Inform doctor if you have a history of keloid formation or bleeding disorders.
  • Arrive with clean skin around the mole area without heavy foundation or powder.
STEP 02

During the Procedure

  • Localized micro-injection of local anesthetic ensures 100% painless ablation.
  • Ultra-fine focused CO2 laser vaporizes mole cells layer by layer with microscopic precision.
  • Simultaneous vascular coagulation ensures a completely bloodless field.
  • Surrounding healthy dermal tissue is completely preserved with minimal margin impact.
STEP 03

Post-Care & Recovery

  • Keep the tiny sterile dressing intact for 24 hours after the procedure.
  • A dry protective micro-crust will form and shed naturally in 5–7 days.
  • Apply prescribed antibiotic ointment twice daily until the crust separates.
  • Strictly apply sunscreen on the treated spot to avoid temporary post-inflammatory color.

Common Questions

Laser Mole Removal FAQs

Is laser mole removal painful?

The area is thoroughly numbed with a local anesthetic, so you feel virtually no pain during the laser ablation.

Will there be a scar after mole removal?

Laser ablation vaporizes tissue layer by layer with minimal thermal damage, resulting in minimal to no scarring compared to surgical excision.

How long does it take to heal after mole removal?

A tiny crust or scab forms within 24 hours and falls off naturally in 5 to 7 days, leaving behind fresh, pink skin that gradually blends with your natural tone.

Can multiple moles be removed in one session?

Yes, multiple benign moles and skin tags can be safely treated and removed in a single clinical session.
