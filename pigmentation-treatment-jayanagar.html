Clinical Care Protocol

What to Expect: Preparation, Session & Recovery

STEP 01

Pre-Care Preparation

  • Avoid unprotected sun exposure and outdoor sports for 1 week prior to session.
  • Discontinue prescription hydroquinone, tretinoin, and glycolic peels 3 days before.
  • Do not bleach facial hair, wax, or thread within 5 days of your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean, bare skin free of concealers, foundations, or chemical sunblocks.
STEP 02

During the Procedure

  • Woods lamp & dermatoscope examination to distinguish epidermal vs dermal melasma.
  • Q-Switched Nd:YAG 1064nm laser toning pulses pass harmlessly through surface skin.
  • Photoacoustic acoustic shockwaves break melanin clusters into micro-debris without wounds.
  • Application of medical brightening serum and broad-spectrum zinc oxide protection.
STEP 03

Post-Care & Recovery

  • Strict adherence to broad-spectrum SPF 50+ PA++++ reapplication every 3 hours.
  • Zero peeling or wound care needed; skin looks instantly cleaner and refreshed.
  • Avoid direct kitchen steam, facial saunas, and hot baths for 48 hours.
  • Noticeable lightening and tonal uniformity develops progressively over 3–5 sessions.

Common Questions

Pigmentation FAQs

How long does it take to see results for melasma?

Melasma is a chronic condition, but with our laser toning and medical peels, most patients see significant lightening within 3-4 months of consistent treatment.

Are chemical peels safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, we use customized AHA and Glow peels that are specifically chosen based on your skin's sensitivity to ensure brightening without irritation.

Will the pigmentation return after treatment?

Maintenance is key. We provide a customized post-treatment skincare regimen, including high-quality sun protection, to prevent recurrence.

What is Carbon Laser Peel (Hollywood Peel)?

It's a quick, painless procedure that uses a layer of liquid carbon and laser energy to exfoliate the skin, clear pores, and provide an instant glow.
