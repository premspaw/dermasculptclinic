Common Questions

Facial Rejuvenation FAQs

Will Wrinkle-Smoothing Care make my face look frozen?

No. When administered by an expert like Dr. Deepak Devakar, Wrinkle-Smoothing Care provides natural-looking results that soften wrinkles while preserving your facial expressions.

How long does facial volume care last?

Depending on the product used and the area treated, facial volume care typically lasts between 6 to 18 months. Maintenance sessions can help sustain the results.

Is there any downtime after the session?

Downtime is minimal. Some patients may notice slight redness or swelling in the treated area, which usually settles within a few hours to a couple of days.

Are these procedures painful?

We use comfort-focused techniques and can apply topical numbing cream to make the procedure as comfortable as possible.
